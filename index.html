Our Operational Framework

The Four Pillars of Project KWENTA

Designed to empower school heads, bookkeepers, and disbursing officers in managing public education funds with accuracy and integrity.

Pillar 1

Transparent Finances

Open access to financial reporting templates, real-time liquidation monitoring, and regular disclosure of division fund allocation.

  • Clear liquidation guidelines
  • Public compliance status
Pillar 2

Responsible Stewardship

Strict adherence to COA rules, DBM circulars, and DepEd financial orders ensuring every peso directly benefits learner outcomes.

  • Internal Audit pre-checks
  • BIR Tax compliance aid
Pillar 3

Empowered Schools

Continuous capacity building, coaching, and 1-on-1 technical assistance for non-IUs and implementing unit financial staff.

  • Helpdesk TA ticketing system
  • Hands-on workshops
Pillar 4

Sustainable Impact

Establishing institutionalized financial systems that ensure timely school MOOE disbursements and zero disallowances.

  • Automated financial tools
  • Long-term compliance tracking

Frequently Asked TA Topics

When is the deadline for monthly MOOE liquidation?

Per DepEd guidelines, MOOE liquidation reports must be submitted on or before the 5th day of the succeeding month to allow timely replenishment.

What are the primary documents for travel liquidation?

Approved Travel Order, Certificate of Appearance, Itinerary of Travel, Official Receipts/Tickets, and Certificate of Travel Completed.

How to request tax exemption certificates for suppliers?

Submit Form 2307 requests directly via the Accounting Helpdesk or download the automated generator tool below.
